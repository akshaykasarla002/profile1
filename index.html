03 / PRACTICE CASE STUDY

Illustrative export compliance process

Scenario: a company in Germany prepares to export machinery to a customer in India. This is a learning scenario, not a claim of a live implementation. Select a step to see what happens in it.

01

Sales document created

An export-relevant sales transaction is created in the source ERP system.
What happens
A sales order for the machinery is entered in SAP SD with the sold-to and ship-to parties, material, quantity, value and Incoterms.
Data that matters later
Partner names and addresses, the destination country, the commodity code and the country of origin of the product. Poor data here causes false blocks later.
Outcome
The order is saved and the delivery can be planned, but it is not yet cleared for shipping.
02

Data transferred to GTS

Relevant business partner, product and transaction data is transferred for checks.
What happens
The ERP system sends the order or delivery, together with the master data it depends on, to SAP GTS through the integration interface.
In SAP GTS
GTS creates its own trade document for the transaction and starts the compliance, legal control and customs checks that are configured.
Outcome
The transaction is waiting for check results. Depending on the setup, the ERP system holds the delivery until GTS answers.
03

Compliance screening

Applicable SPL and embargo checks are performed based on configured rules and data.
What happens
The customer, ship-to party and other partners are screened against sanctioned party lists, and the destination is checked against embargo rules.
In SAP GTS
A name that is similar to a listed party produces a hit. The document is blocked and appears in the compliance officer’s worklist.
Outcome
No hit: the document passes. Hit: the officer reviews it, decides whether it is a false positive, and releases or rejects it. The decision is logged.
04

Legal control assessment

Export-control requirements and any required license are evaluated.
What happens
The machinery is checked against its classification, for example whether it falls under a dual-use control list, and the destination and partners are taken into account.
In SAP GTS
License determination decides whether a license is needed. If so, the license check looks for a valid license with enough remaining value or quantity.
Outcome
Most machinery needs no license and passes. If a license is required but missing or expired, the document stays blocked until one is assigned.
05

Customs processing

Relevant export declaration information is prepared and processed with the customs system.
What happens
GTS builds the export declaration from the delivery data: commodity code, value, weight, Incoterms and the parties involved.
In SAP GTS
The declaration is sent electronically to the national customs system, for example ATLAS in Germany, and customs answers with status messages.
Outcome
Customs releases the export and returns a reference number (MRN) and an export accompanying document that travels with the goods.
06

Release and follow-up

The transaction proceeds when applicable compliance and customs requirements are satisfied.
What happens
GTS sends the overall status back to the ERP system, which can now release the delivery and post the goods issue.
Follow-up
When the goods leave the customs territory, an exit confirmation is received and the customs status is updated. Documents are kept for audits and reporting.
Outcome
The shipment goes out with a complete, traceable compliance and customs record.
